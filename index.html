The idea behind HindOS

Less noise.
More room to think.

Operating systems are the foundation of everything we do on a computer. HindOS aims to make that foundation feel approachable and coherent, without losing the freedom and flexibility that make Linux special.

◒

Designed with intention

A restrained visual language, balanced spacing, and a desktop concept that keeps attention on your work.

⌘

Open at its core

Built around a Debian Bookworm-based Linux foundation and the wider open-source ecosystem.

✳

Made to keep evolving

A project shaped through experimentation, testing, feedback, and careful improvements over time.

The experience

A desktop that feels considered.

The visual direction is a starting point. Each capability will be described as available only when it has been implemented and tested in the actual OS.

01 / The interface

Calm, clear, and familiar.

Translucent surfaces, elegant typography, rounded windows, and a consistent desktop language — designed as a cohesive whole, not a collection of disconnected parts.

Desktop conceptGlass-inspired UIClear hierarchy
02 / The foundation

Linux, with room to shape.

Start with the flexibility of a Debian-based system, then develop the HindOS experience through a desktop environment, system tools, and a carefully tested installer.

Hardware & compatibility

Designed with everyday computers in mind.

The ambition is to make HindOS useful on practical hardware. Final minimum requirements should be based on testing a bootable image, not estimates.
